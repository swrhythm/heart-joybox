# ❤️ HEART JOYBOX

## Press the button. Take your verse.

---

### What the light means

| The light is… | It means… |
|---|---|
| **On, steady** | Ready — press the button |
| **Flickering** | Printing — just a moment |
| **Blinking fast** (just switched on) | Starting up — wait 30 seconds |
| **Two blinks, pause, two blinks** | Check the printer is switched on |
| **Three blinks, pause, three blinks** | Out of paper, or the lid is open |
| **Slow on-off, on-off** | Needs attention — call the organiser |
| **Off** | No power — check the plug |

---

### If something is wrong, try this first

**1.** Unplug the small board.
**2.** Count to ten.
**3.** Plug it back in.
**4.** Wait until the light is steady (about 30 seconds).
**5.** Press the button.

You cannot break it by doing this.

---

### Out of paper?

1. Open the printer lid.
2. Drop the roll in so the paper comes off the **top** and the **shiny side
   faces up**.
3. Leave a few centimetres hanging out.
4. Close the lid until it clicks.

The light goes steady on its own. Nothing else to do.

---

### Nothing is printing and the light looks fine?

**Hold the button down for five seconds.** It prints a status slip.
Keep that slip — it tells the organiser exactly what is wrong.
